The Rust Standard Library is compiled into every GeoBrix Rust kernel.

"The Rust Standard Library is dual-licensed under Apache 2.0 and MIT terms." (COPYRIGHT-library.html, "Short version".)
COPYRIGHT-library.html is the pinned Rust toolchain's share/doc/rust/COPYRIGHT-library.html: the standard library's own
notice, with the notices of the files and crates it includes (compiler_builtins, hashbrown, ...). LICENSE-MIT and
LICENSE-APACHE are the same toolchain's share/doc/rust/licenses/MIT.txt and Apache-2.0.txt, the MIT text with its
copyright line filled in as COPYRIGHT-library.html states it. All three are identical in the toolchain's
aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu, x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu and aarch64-apple-darwin builds.
